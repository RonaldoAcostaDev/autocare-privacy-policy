Privacy Policy

AutoCare

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Compose R.A Studio developed AutoCare as a car maintenance log for drivers. It lets the user record their vehicles, fuel fill-ups and battery charges, services and repairs, and other vehicle expenses; receive maintenance reminders by mileage or by date; keep insurance, inspection, registration, and driver's license documents with their expiration dates; attach photos or PDF files of receipts; view fuel economy and spending statistics; export PDF and CSV reports; and make backups.

All the information entered in the application is stored only on the user's device. The application does not have its own server (backend), does not require creating an account, does not ask for user registration, and does not use any authentication method, such as logging in with Google or any other provider. Its maintenance features work without an internet connection.

To remain free of charge, the application shows advertisements served by Google AdMob and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and record privacy choices about advertising where the law requires it. These services never receive the vehicles, records, documents, or attachments recorded in the application. The application does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own.

Information privacy and security are a priority for Compose R.A Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application handles, where it is kept, and what choices and rights the user has.

Information Stored on the Device

The information created by the user, including, but not limited to:

  • Vehicles: name or nickname, type, make, model, year, license plate, fuel type, color in the application, and odometer readings
  • Fill-ups and charges: date, odometer, amount of fuel or energy, price, total paid, fuel grade, gas station, and notes
  • Services and repairs: date, odometer, work done, shop, cost, and notes
  • Expenses: category, amount, date, description, and notes
  • Reminders: the job or title, the mileage or date it is due, the advance notice, and whether it is active
  • Documents: type, name, number, insurer or issuer, expiration date, and whether it is a personal document
  • Attachments: photos and PDF files of receipts, invoices, or policies that the user adds to a record or document
  • Settings: currency, distance, volume and fuel economy units, theme, language, notification time, and default advance notice

is stored exclusively in the application's private storage on the user's device. Compose R.A Studio does not collect, store, process, or have access to this information.

The data remains under the exclusive control of the user, unless they decide to share a report or make a backup using the features available in the application.

To suggest the currency, units, and number formats when the application is set up, it reads the country code of the SIM card and of the mobile network, and the region configured on the phone. This does not use the device's location (GPS), does not require any permission, happens on the device, and is not sent anywhere.

Photos and Files

Attachments are optional and are added only when the user chooses to:

  • Take a photo: the application opens the device's camera app, which saves the picture directly into the application's private storage. AutoCare does not request the camera permission and never accesses the camera on its own.
  • Pick from gallery: Android's photo picker gives the application access only to the image the user selects.
  • PDF file: Android's file picker gives the application access only to the file the user selects.

A copy of each attachment is kept in the application's private storage, so it remains available even if the original is deleted. It is deleted when the user removes the attachment, shortly after the user deletes the record it belongs to (so an accidental deletion can still be undone), or when the application's data is cleared.

What the Application Does Not Do

AutoCare:

  • Does not require creating an account
  • Does not ask for the user's name, email, or phone number to use the application
  • Does not use authentication through Google, Facebook, or other services
  • Does not sync the recorded information with its own servers or any other server
  • Does not track trips or the vehicle's location: mileage is entered by the user
  • Does not connect to the vehicle or to any diagnostic device
  • Does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own
  • Does not access the device's location (GPS), contacts, microphone, or SMS messages
  • Does not access the photo gallery or files beyond the items the user selects
  • Does not send, sell, or share the vehicles, records, documents, or attachments recorded by the user with anyone, including advertisers

Third-Party Services

The application uses the following third-party services. They may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The application shows full-screen advertisements (interstitials) served by Google AdMob. They never appear when the application opens, during the welcome and setup screens, or during the first 24 hours after the application is installed. They appear only occasionally, after the user has saved several entries, such as a fill-up, a service, an expense, an odometer update, a reminder, a document, or a vehicle, with a minimum time between two advertisements. They are shown at a natural pause, right after an entry is saved and its screen closes, and never on top of the share menu or in the middle of a task.

To serve and measure advertisements, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud, and, where the user allows it, personalize them, AdMob may collect:

  • The device's advertising ID
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived
  • Device and operating system information
  • Interactions with advertisements, such as impressions and clicks
  • Performance and diagnostic information of the advertising SDK, including crash logs

On devices that support them, AdMob may also use Android's advertising APIs of the Privacy Sandbox (such as Topics and Attribution), which the user can manage or turn off in the device settings.

The vehicles, records, documents, and attachments recorded in the application are never shared with AdMob and are not used to choose advertisements. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google User Messaging Platform (Privacy Choices)

The application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent management platform provided by Google, to request and record privacy choices about advertising as required in each region. These choices are stored on the device and passed to Google's advertising services.

Google Play Billing

The application includes Google Play's billing library so that optional in-app purchases can be offered through Google Play. If a purchase is offered and the user makes it, it is processed entirely by Google Play: payment details are entered in Google Play and are never received by the application or by Compose R.A Studio. Google handles the purchase, its receipt, and any refund according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Sharing Initiated by the User

Some features open other applications. They are used only when the user requests them, and the user can review the content before sending it:

  • Reports (PDF and CSV): the maintenance report and the spreadsheet can be shared through the Android share menu or saved in the location the user chooses.
  • Attachments: a PDF attachment can be opened in another application installed on the device, chosen by the user.
  • Contact and sharing buttons: "Contact support" opens the user's email application with a prepared message that includes the application version, the Android version, and the device model; "Share the app" and "Rate the app" open the share menu or Google Play. Nothing is sent until the user confirms it.

Once information is shared, it is handled by the application or service the user chose, according to its own privacy policy. To share a report, the application creates a temporary copy of the file in its private cache, which Android may clear at any time and which is deleted when the application's data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Backups

From More → Backup and restore, the user can create a backup file (.acbackup) with all the information of the application: vehicles, records, reminders, documents, attachments, and settings. The file is created only when the user requests it and is saved in the location the user chooses, such as the device's storage or a cloud storage service. If that location is synchronized with a cloud storage service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

The backup file is not encrypted, so it should be kept in a safe place. Restoring a backup replaces all the information currently stored in the application; a copy of the previous information is kept on the device so the last restore can be undone.

If backup is enabled in the device's Android settings, Android may also include the application's data in the backup of the user's Google account, or copy it when moving to a new device. That backup is managed by Google under the user's account, and Compose R.A Studio has no access to it. It can be turned off from the device's backup settings.

Maintenance Reminders and Notifications

When maintenance alerts are turned on, the application checks on the device, once a day at the time the user chooses, which reminders are due soon or overdue and which documents are about to expire or have expired, and shows a notification. These notifications may include the job or document name, the vehicle name, and the remaining mileage or days, and may appear on the lock screen depending on the device's notification settings.

Alerts can be turned off at any time in Settings → Notifications → Maintenance alerts, and notifications can also be disabled from the device settings.

Data Security

The information is kept in the application's private storage, which Android isolates from other applications, and benefits from the storage encryption of the device where it is available. To protect it further, it is recommended to use a screen lock on the device and to keep backup files and exported reports in a safe place.

Data Retention and Deletion

The information stored on the device remains there until the user deletes it. The user can:

  • Edit or delete fill-ups, services, expenses, reminders, documents, and attachments from within the application. Deleted entries can be restored for a few seconds with "Undo"
  • Delete a vehicle together with its whole history, or archive it to keep the history
  • Delete the sample car and its data from Settings → Data
  • Delete backup files and exported reports from the location where they were saved
  • Delete all the information by clearing the application's data from the device settings or by uninstalling the application

Backups, reports, and other files saved or shared by the user remain wherever the user saved or sent them until the user deletes them. Because Compose R.A Studio does not operate servers or user accounts, it does not hold a copy of the user's information that it could access or delete.

Information collected by Google's services is retained according to Google's own policies and retention settings. For requests about that information, the user can contact Compose R.A Studio, which will help direct them to the right place.
